Book I, Proposition 11: Centres of Gravity in Similar Triangles (I)¶

Proposition 11. If two triangles be similar, and two points within them be similarly situated with respect to the triangles, and if one point is the centre of gravity of the first triangle, the other point is likewise the centre of gravity of the second triangle.

Proof: Follows directly from Post. 5 (similarly situated centres of gravity in similar figures) and the uniqueness of the centre of gravity.

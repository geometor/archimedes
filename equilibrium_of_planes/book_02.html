Book II: Centres of Gravity of Parabolic Segments (Propositions 1–10)¶

In Book II (10 Propositions), Archimedes combines Book I with the Quadrature of the Parabola (archimedes:quadrature-of-parabola) to determine the exact centre of gravity of any parabolic segment and parabolic frustum:

  • Proposition 1: Two parabolic segments placed on a balance are treated as plane laminae via Book I, Props. 6–7.

  • Propositions 2–7: Recursive inscription of triangles (“known manner” triangleABC + triangleADB + triangleBEC + …) proves that the centre of gravity of any parabolic segment lies on its diameter AO (Prop. 2), that similar-like exhaustion polygons have centres of gravity approaching that of the segment (Props. 3–6), and that the ratio in which the centre of gravity divides the diameter is constant across all parabolic segments (Prop. 7).

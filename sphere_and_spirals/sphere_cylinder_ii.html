On the Sphere and Cylinder II: Spherical Segments & Eutocius’s Cubic Intersection¶

  • Book II, Proposition 2 (archimedes:sphere-and-cylinder:ii-2): Every spherical segment of height in a sphere of radius equals the cone having the same base as the segment and altitude , whence .

  • Book II, Proposition 4: To cut a given sphere by a plane so that the volumes of the segments have to one another a given ratio . Archimedes’ analysis reduces the division of diameter to the cubic proportion:

    \[(a - x) : b = c^2 : x^2, \qquad \text{i.e.} \qquad x^2(a - x) = b c^2,\]

    promising to append its synthesis and diorismos at the end.

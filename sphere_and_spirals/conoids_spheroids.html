On Conoids and Spheroids: Paraboloids, Hyperboloids & Spheroids¶

In 32 propositions (archimedes:conoids-and-spheroids), Archimedes extends exhaustion by inscribed and circumscribed cylinder-slices (“gnomons” of equal thickness) to affine-scaled quadric surfaces of revolution:

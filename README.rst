GEOMETOR • archimedes
=====================

Scholarly Digital Scriptorium Book Codex of *The Works of Archimedes* (ed. T. L. Heath, Cambridge University Press, 1897) and *The Method of Archimedes, Recently Discovered by Heiberg* (ed. T. L. Heath, Cambridge, 1912 Supplement), paired with Johan Ludvig Heiberg's *Archimedis Opera Omnia* (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1880–1881 / 1910–1915).

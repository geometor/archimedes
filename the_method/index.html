Author: Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287–212 BC)

“For certain things first became clear to me by a mechanical method, although they had to be demonstrated by geometry afterwards because their investigation by the said method did not furnish an actual demonstration.”

—Archimedes, The Method Addressed to Eratosthenes

Table of Contents¶

Prefatory Letter to Eratosthenes & Statical Lemmas 1–11

“ARCHIMEDES TO ERATOSTHENES GREETING.

Proposition 1: Heuristic Slice Quadrature of the Parabola

Let ABC be a segment of a parabola bounded by the straight line AC and the parabola ABC. Bisect AC at D, draw DBE parallel to the diameter, and join AB, BC. Then shall the segment ABC be of the triangle ABC.

Proposition 2: Mechanical Discovery of the Sphere Volume & Surface

  1. Any sphere is (in respect of solid content) four times the cone with base equal to a great circle of the sphere and height equal to its radius; and (2) the cylinder with base equal to a great circle of the sphere and height equal to the diameter is :eq:`1frac{1}{2}` times the sphere.

Propositions 3–15: Conoids, Centroids, the Ungula & the Bicylinder

Propositions 3–11 (pp. 21–38): Apply the identical slice-lever engine to discover the volumes of spheroids (Prop. 3), paraboloids (Prop. 4), centroids of paraboloid segments ( along the axis, Prop. 5!), centroids of hemispheres ( from pole to centre, i.e. from the base, Prop. 6!), spherical segments (Props. 7, 9), spheroid segments (Props. 8, 10), and hyperboloid segments & centroids (Prop. 11).

Proposition 7: Volume of a Segment of a Sphere
==============================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 29

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 7.** Any segment of a sphere has to the cone which has the same base as the segment and the same height the ratio which the sum of the radius of the sphere and the height of the complementary segment has to the height of the complementary segment (matching *Sphere and Cylinder* II.2).

Archimedes discovers the spherical-segment cubature formula mechanically by balancing sections of the segment and an auxiliary cone against a cylinder along the diameter lever.

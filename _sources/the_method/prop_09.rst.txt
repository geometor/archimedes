Proposition 9: Centre of Gravity of a Segment of a Sphere
=========================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 33

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 9.** The :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of any segment of a sphere (of height :eq:`h` on a sphere of radius :eq:`r`) lies on its axis, dividing :eq:`h` from vertex to base in the ratio :eq:`(4r - h) : (2r - h) \cdot 2` — i.e., at distance :eq:`\frac{h(4r - h)}{4(3r - h)}` from the base.

.. qed::

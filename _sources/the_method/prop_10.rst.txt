Proposition 10: Centre of Gravity of a Segment of a Spheroid
============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 35

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 10.** The :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of any segment of a :term:`spheroid <sphairoeides>` cut off by a plane perpendicular to the axis divides the axis of the segment in the identical algebraic ratio to the corresponding spherical segment of Proposition 9.

.. qed::

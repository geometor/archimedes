Proposition 5: Centre of Gravity of a Paraboloid Segment
========================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 25

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 5.** The :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of a segment of a :term:`right-angled conoid <orthogonion-konoeides>` (paraboloid of revolution) cut off by a plane at right angles to the axis lies on the axis, dividing it so that the portion adjacent to the vertex is **double** the remaining portion adjacent to the base (:eq:`2 : 1` from vertex to base).

By hanging the paraboloid's circular slices at the fixed end of the lever against a cone whose centre of gravity divides the axis in ratio :eq:`3 : 1`, Archimedes locates the paraboloid centroid at :eq:`\frac{2}{3}` of the axis from the vertex.

.. qed::

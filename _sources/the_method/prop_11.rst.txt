Proposition 11: Hyperboloid Segment Volume & Centre of Gravity
==============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 36

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 11.** Determines by the mechanical slice-lever method both the volume ratio (to the inscribed cone) and the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` along the axis of any segment of an :term:`obtuse-angled conoid <amblygonion-konoeides>` (two-sheeted hyperboloid of revolution), matching the results later proved by exhaustion in *On Conoids and Spheroids*.

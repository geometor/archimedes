Proposition 12: Mechanical Slice Discovery of the Cylindrical Ungula
====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 38

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 12 (Cylindrical Ungula — Mechanical Method).** If in a right prism with square base a cylinder be inscribed with its bases in opposite squares and its curved surface touching the four lateral faces, and a plane be drawn through one side of the square base and the centre of the opposite square, the :term:`cylindrical ungula <ungula>` (hoof) cut off is **one-sixth** of the whole prism (:eq:`V = \frac{2}{3} r^2 h`).

First promised in the Preface to :person:`Eratosthenes <eratosthenes>`, Archimedes balances vertical rectangular slices of the ungula against corresponding slices of an auxiliary prism on a lever—exhibiting a curved solid equal to a purely rectilinear fraction of its bounding box without :eq:`\pi`.

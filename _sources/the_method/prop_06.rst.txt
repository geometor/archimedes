Proposition 6: Centre of Gravity of a Hemisphere
================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 27

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 6.** The :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of any hemisphere lies on its axis, dividing the radius in the ratio :eq:`5 : 3` from the vertex (pole) to the centre of the sphere (i.e. at distance :eq:`\frac{3}{8} r` from the base).

Balancing the hemisphere together with an inscribed cone in situ against a larger cylinder transported to the opposite arm yields the exact centroid ratio :eq:`5 : 3`.

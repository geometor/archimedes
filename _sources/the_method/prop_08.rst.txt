Proposition 8: Volume of a Segment of a Spheroid
================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 31

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 8.** Any segment of a :term:`spheroid <sphairoeides>` cut off by a plane at right angles to the axis bears to the cone with the same base and height the ratio of half the axis plus the height of the complementary segment to the height of the complementary segment (extending Prop. 7 to ellipsoids of revolution).

Book of Lemmas (*Liber Assumptorum*) — Analytical Summary
=========================================================

*Scope*: **Heiberg Vol. II, pp. 426–445** · **Heath (1897), pp. 301–318** · *Propositions 1–15*

Planar Gemstones of Circle Tangency, Curvilinear Quadrature & :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unlike the exhaustion treatises, the *Book of Lemmas* achieves **instantaneous exact quadratures** of curvilinear figures (:term:`Arbelos <arbelos>` in Prop. 4, :term:`Salinon <salinon>` in Prop. 14) by algebraic cancellation of semicircular areas (:just:`Eucl. XII. 2`), alongside projective/homothetic circle-contact invariants (Props. 1, 5, 6), kinematic :term:`neusis <neusis>` angle trisection (Prop. 8), orthogonal-chord quadrance conservation (Prop. 11), and golden-ratio decagon geometry (Prop. 15).

Complete Register of all 15 Propositions
----------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 10 30 60

   * - Prop.
     - Theme
     - Exact Geometric Statement
   * - **1**
     - Homothetic Tangent Circles
     - Parallel diameters :g:`CD \parallel EF` in tangent circles have collinear extremities :g:`ADF` through contact :g:`A`
   * - **2**
     - Semicircle Tangents
     - Secant :g:`AT` to intersection of tangents at :g:`B, D` bisects altitude :g:`DE` (:eq:`DF = FE`)
   * - **3**
     - Equal-Arc Chord Lemma
     - Reflection of altitude foot :eq:`AN = ND` gives :eq:`BQ = BD` for equal arcs :eq:`PQ = PA`
   * - **4**
     - **The Arbelos** (*ἄρβηλος*)
     - Curvilinear area on :eq:`AB = AN + NB` equals the circle on tangent altitude :g:`PN`
   * - **5**
     - **Archimedes' Twin Circles**
     - Circles tangent to :g:`CD`, outer semicircle, and either inner semicircle are equal: :eq:`d = \frac{AC \cdot CB}{AB}`
   * - **6**
     - **Inscribed Arbelos Circle**
     - Circle tangent to all 3 semicircles satisfies :eq:`GH : AB = \lambda : (1 + \lambda + \lambda^2)` (:eq:`6 : 19` for :eq:`3 : 2`)
   * - **7**
     - Square Circles
     - Circumcircle of a square is double its incircle (:eq:`2 : 1`)
   * - **8**
     - **Neusis Angle Trisection**
     - Inserting :eq:`BC = r` between circle and extended diameter :g:`DOE` gives :eq:`\text{arc } AE = 3 \cdot \text{arc } BD`
   * - **9**
     - Orthogonal Secants
     - Arc sum identity for perpendicular chords
   * - **10**
     - Tangent-Secant Bisection
     - Harmonic/angular properties of tangent & secant chords (cf. Apollonius *Contacts*)
   * - **11**
     - **Orthogonal Chords Quadrance**
     - If :eq:`AB \perp CD` at :g:`O`, then :eq:`AO^2 + BO^2 + CO^2 + DO^2 = 4R^2`
   * - **12**
     - Semicircle Polar Orthocenter
     - Tangents at :g:`P, Q` meeting at :g:`T` and chords :g:`AQ \cap BP = R` satisfy :eq:`TR \perp AB`
   * - **13**
     - Diameter Projections
     - Perpendiculars from diameter ends onto a chord cut off equal outer segments (:eq:`CN = DM`)
   * - **14**
     - **The Salinon** (*σάλινον*)
     - Symmetric 4-semicircle salt-cellar equals the circle on axial segment :g:`CF`
   * - **15**
     - **Pentagon, Decagon &** :eq:`\phi`
     - Pentagon/decagon secant :g:`CDE` meets diameter at :g:`E` with :eq:`EM = R` via :just:`Eucl. XIII. 9` (:eq:`\phi`)

Proposition 11: Orthogonal Chords Quadrance Invariant
=====================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 311–315 · Heiberg II, 438–442
   :page: 312

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 11 (Orthogonal Chords Quadrance Invariant).** If two chords :g:`AB`, :g:`CD` in a circle intersect at right angles in a point :g:`O`, not being the centre, then:

   .. math::

      AO^2 + BO^2 + CO^2 + DO^2 = (\text{diameter})^2 = 4 R^2.

*Proof*: Proved cleanly via a diameter :g:`CE` drawn perpendicular to :g:`AB` (or through the centre), applying Thales's theorem (:just:`Eucl. III. 31`) and the Pythagorean theorem (:just:`Eucl. I. 47`)—a pure Rational Trigonometry quadrance identity (:cite:`geometor:wildberger`).

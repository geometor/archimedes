Proposition 3: Chord Ratios & Half-Angle Arcs
=============================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 301–304 · Heiberg II, 426–430
   :page: 303

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 3 (Chord Ratios & Half-Angle Arcs).** Let :g:`P` be any point on a segment of a circle whose base is :g:`AB`, and let :g:`PN` be perpendicular to :g:`AB`. Take :g:`D` on :g:`AB` so that :eq:`AN = ND`. If :g:`PQ` be an arc equal to the arc :g:`PA`, and :g:`BQ` be joined, then :g:`BQ`, :g:`BD` are equal.

*Proof*: Joining :g:`PA`, :g:`PD`, :g:`PQ`, we have :eq:`PD = PA = PQ` and equal angles subtended by equal arcs, yielding :eq:`BQ = BD` and the proportion :eq:`(AB + BD) : BP = QB : PQ` in the half-angle chord geometry later utilized by Ptolemy.

.. qed::

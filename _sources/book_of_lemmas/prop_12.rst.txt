Proposition 12: Polar-Orthocenter Property of Semicircle Tangents
=================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 311–315 · Heiberg II, 438–442
   :page: 313

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 12 (Polar-Orthocenter Property of Semicircle Tangents).** If :g:`AB` be the diameter of a semicircle, and :g:`TP`, :g:`TQ` the tangents to it from any point :g:`T`, and if :g:`AQ`, :g:`BP` be joined meeting in :g:`R`, then :g:`TR` produced is perpendicular to :g:`AB`.

*Proof*: Joining :g:`AP`, :g:`BQ` gives right angles at :g:`P` and :g:`Q` (:just:`Eucl. III. 31`), so :g:`R` is the orthocenter of the triangle formed by :g:`AB` and lines :g:`AQ`, :g:`BP`; pole-polar symmetry with :eq:`TP = TQ` confirms :g:`TR \perp AB`.

.. qed::

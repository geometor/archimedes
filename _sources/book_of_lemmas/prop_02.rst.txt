Proposition 2: Semicircle Tangents & Altitude Bisection
=======================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 301–304 · Heiberg II, 426–430
   :page: 302

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 2 (Semicircle Tangents & Altitude Bisection).** Let :g:`AB` be the diameter of a semicircle, and let the tangents to it at :g:`B` and at any other point :g:`D` on it meet in :g:`T`. If now :g:`DE` be drawn perpendicular to :g:`AB`, and if :g:`AT`, :g:`DE` meet in :g:`F`, then :eq:`DF = FE`.

*Proof*: Producing :g:`AD` to meet the tangent at :g:`B` in :g:`H`, since :g:`\angle ADB` is right (:just:`Eucl. III. 31`) and :eq:`TD = TB` (tangents from :g:`T`), :g:`T` is the midpoint of :g:`BH` (:eq:`TH = TB`). By similarity of triangles cut by the parallel lines :g:`DE \parallel HB`, :g:`AF` bisects :g:`DE` at :g:`F`, whence :eq:`DF = FE`.

.. qed::

King Gelon II of Syracuse
=========================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Γέλων*
* **Role**: Co-regent of Syracuse & addressee of The Sand-Reckoner
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Sand-Reckoner I.1–IV (Heath 1897, pp. 221–232)

Son of Hiero II; addressed directly ('King Gelon', βασιλεῦ Γέλων) throughout The Sand-Reckoner, where Archimedes demonstrates his myriad-octad number system capable of exceeding the grains of sand filling Aristarchus's cosmos.

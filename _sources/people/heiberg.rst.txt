Johan Ludvig Heiberg
====================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Iohannes Ludovicus Heiberg*
* **Role**: Critical Teubner editor & discoverer of the Archimedes Palimpsest (1906)
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Heath 1897 Pref.; Heath 1912 Introductory Note (pp. 5–11)

Established the definitive critical Greek edition Archimedis Opera Omnia cum commentariis Eutocii (Teubner, 1st ed. 1880–1881; 2nd ed. 1910–1915) and in 1906 deciphered Codex C (the Constantinople Palimpsest), recovering the lost Greek text of The Method, Stomachion, and On Floating Bodies.

Thābit ibn Qurra
================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Θάβιτ ἰμπν Κούρρα (ثابت بن قرة)*
* **Role**: Sabian mathematician & Arabic translator of Archimedes' Book of Lemmas
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Book of Lemmas Props. 1–15 (Heath 1897, pp. 301–318)

Working in Baghdad's House of Wisdom tradition, translated Archimedes' Sphere and Cylinder, Measurement of a Circle, and the fifteen propositions of the Book of Lemmas (Kitāb al-Maʾkhūdhāt / Liber Assumptorum), preserving the Arbelos, Twin Circles, Neusis Trisection, and Salinon.

Johannes Kepler
===============

* **Original Name / Form**: *Ioannes Keplerus*
* **Role**: Astronomer & geometer who re-proved all 13 Archimedean Solids
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Unit 7: Thirteen Archimedean Semi-Regular Solids Concordance

In Harmonices Mundi Book II (1619), independently reconstructed, proved the completeness of, and assigned the modern Latin names (cuboctahedron, icosidodecahedron, snub cube, snub dodecahedron, etc.) to all thirteen Archimedean semi-regular solids attested by Pappus.

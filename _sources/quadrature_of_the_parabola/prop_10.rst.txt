Proposition 10: Lever Bounds for an Oblique Trapezium Strip (I)
===============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 241

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 10.** If a trapezium suspended beneath :g:`OB` has parallel vertical sides through :g:`E` and :g:`C` and oblique non-parallel sides meeting at a point on the opposite side of the vertical, its balancing weight :g:`P` at :g:`A` satisfies the analogous two-sided lever inequality between :g:`OE` and :g:`OC` (:just:`Equil. I. 15`).

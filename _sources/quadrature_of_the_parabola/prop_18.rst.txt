Proposition 18: Vertex & Diameter-Height of a Parabolic Segment
===============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 246–252 · Heiberg II, 336–353
   :page: 246

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 18.** If :g:`Qq` be the base of a parabolic segment and :g:`V` its midpoint, and if the diameter through :g:`V` meet the parabola at :g:`P` (the vertex where the tangent is parallel to :g:`Qq`), then :g:`P` is the point of greatest perpendicular distance from :g:`Qq`, and the diameter through the midpoint of :g:`QV` meets the tangent at :g:`P` and the curve in points cutting off :eq:`\frac{3}{4}` of :g:`PV` along the diameter.

To demonstrate the quadrature purely geometrically without mechanical postulates, Archimedes opens Part II by defining the *base*, *vertex*, and *height* of any segment.

Proposition 11: Lever Bounds for an Oblique Trapezium Strip (II)
================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 241

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 11.** Completes the case analysis for any trapezium bounded by two vertical parallels suspended from :g:`OB`, proving that in all inclinations the balancing area at :g:`A` lies strictly between the two endpoint-moment bounds.

.. qed::

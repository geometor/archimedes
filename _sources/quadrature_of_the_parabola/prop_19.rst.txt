Proposition 19: Quarter-Height Scaling of Secondary Segments
============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 246–252 · Heiberg II, 336–353
   :page: 247

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 19.** If diameters through the midpoints :g:`F`, :g:`G` of half-chords :g:`AB`, :g:`BC` (or :g:`AD`) meet the parabola at secondary vertices :g:`D`, :g:`E`, then the diameter-segment height of each sub-segment is **one-fourth** of the main height (:eq:`BV = 4 \cdot DF` measured along the diameter).

*Proof*: Follows directly from the ordinate-squared property of the parabola (:just:`Prop. 3`), since halving the ordinate quarters the abscissa along the diameter.

.. qed::

Proposition 14: Inscribed & Circumscribed Trapezium Bounds (Perpendicular Base)
===============================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 243

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 14.** Partitioning a right-angled base :g:`Qq` of the parabolic segment into :eq:`n` equal parts and drawing parallels to the diameter inscribes and circumscribes finite families of rectilinear trapezia inside :g:`\triangle QqT`. Summing the lever inequalities across all :eq:`n` strips compresses the balancing weight of the parabolic segment between inscribed and circumscribed trapezium weights whose difference equals a single strip of width :eq:`\frac{1}{n} Qq`.

.. qed::

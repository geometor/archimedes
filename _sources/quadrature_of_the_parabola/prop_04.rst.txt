Proposition 4: Intercept Ratio Along Parabola Diameter & Tangent (I)
====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 235–246 · Heiberg II, 298–335
   :page: 236

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 4.** If :g:`Qq` be the base of any segment of a parabola, and :g:`P` the vertex of the segment, and if the diameter through any other point :g:`R` meet :g:`Qq` in :g:`O` and the tangent at :g:`Q` in :g:`E`, then :eq:`QO : Oq = ER : RO` (with the axis case when :g:`Qq` is perpendicular to the diameter :g:`PV`).

*Proof*: Combining the ordinate-squared property (:just:`Prop. 3`) with the subtangent identity (:just:`Prop. 2`) and similar triangles yields the linear harmonic-type intercept ratio :eq:`QO : Oq = ER : RO`.

.. qed::

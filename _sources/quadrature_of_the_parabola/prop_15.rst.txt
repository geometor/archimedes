Proposition 15: Inscribed & Circumscribed Trapezium Bounds (Oblique Base)
=========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 244

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 15.** Extends Proposition 14 to any oblique chord :g:`Qq`, showing that partitioning :g:`Qq` into :eq:`n` equal segments and summing the lever inequalities of Proposition 13 compresses the balancing weight of the parabolic segment within a single terminal triangle of base :eq:`\frac{1}{n} Qq`, which can be made smaller than any assigned area (:term:`Archimedes' Lemma <archimedes-lemma>`).

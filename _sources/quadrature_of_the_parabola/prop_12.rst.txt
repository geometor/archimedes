Proposition 12: Balancing a Trapezium Cut from an Inclined Triangle (I)
=======================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 242

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 12.** Applies Propositions 8–11 to a trapezium cut by parallels to the parabola diameter from the enveloping tangent triangle :g:`\triangle QqT` when :g:`Qq` is perpendicular to the diameter, bounding the balancing weight at :g:`A` via the intercept ratio of Proposition 4.

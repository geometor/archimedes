Proposition 20: Inscribed Triangle Exceeds Half the Parabolic Segment
=====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 246–252 · Heiberg II, 336–353
   :page: 248

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 20.** If :g:`ABC` be a parabolic segment with vertex :g:`B`, then :g:`\triangle ABC` is **greater than half** the area of the segment :g:`ABC`.

*Proof*: Since :g:`\triangle ABC` is half of the circumscribed parallelogram formed by the tangent at :g:`B` and the diameters through :g:`A`, :g:`C` (:just:`Eucl. I. 41`), and the parabolic segment lies strictly inside that parallelogram, :g:`\triangle ABC` exceeds half of the segment. Repeated inscription therefore exhausts the segment by :just:`Eucl. X. 1`!

.. qed::

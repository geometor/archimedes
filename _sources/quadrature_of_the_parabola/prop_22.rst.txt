Proposition 22: Monotone Lower Bound of Inscribed Triangle Sums
===============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 246–252 · Heiberg II, 336–353
   :page: 250

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 22.** For any finite sequence of areas :g:`A, B, C, D, \dots` formed at successive stages of the parabolic triangle-tree bisection (:eq:`A = \triangle ABC`, :eq:`B = \frac{1}{4}A`, etc.), their sum :eq:`A + B + C + \dots` is strictly **less** than the area of the parabolic segment.

*Proof*: Each finite partial sum equals the area of a rectilinear polygon strictly inscribed within the parabolic segment (:just:`Prop. 21`), leaving positive residual parabolic sub-segments.

.. qed::

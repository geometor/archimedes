Proposition 5: Intercept Ratio Along Parabola Diameter & Tangent (II)
=====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 235–246 · Heiberg II, 298–335
   :page: 237

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 5.** If :g:`Qq` be the base of any segment of a parabola, :g:`P` the vertex of the segment, and :g:`PV` its diameter, and if the diameter of the parabola through any other point :g:`R` meet :g:`Qq` in :g:`O`, the curve in :g:`R`, and the tangent at :g:`q` (or :g:`Q`) in :g:`E`, then for any oblique chord :g:`Qq`:

   .. math::

      QO : Oq = ER : RO.

Extending Proposition 4 to arbitrary oblique chords, this proportion directly matches the lever-arm ratio needed when suspending parabolic strips from a horizontal beam in Propositions 14–16.

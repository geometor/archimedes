Proposition 6: Balancing a Right-Angled Triangle on a Horizontal Lever
======================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 238

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 6.** Suppose a horizontal balance beam :g:`AOB` with fulcrum :g:`O` and equal arms :eq:`OA = OB`, and suspend a right-angled triangle :g:`BDG` beneath :g:`OB` with side :g:`BD` along the vertical through :g:`B`. Then an area :g:`P` hung at :g:`A` balances :g:`\triangle BDG` in its actual position when :eq:`P = \frac{1}{3} \triangle BDG`.

*Proof*: Because the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of :g:`\triangle BDG` lies horizontally at :eq:`\frac{1}{3} OB` from the fulcrum :g:`O` (:just:`Equil. I. 14`), the Law of the Lever (:just:`Equil. I. 6–7`) gives :eq:`P \cdot OA = \triangle BDG \cdot \frac{1}{3} OB`, whence :eq:`P = \frac{1}{3} \triangle BDG`.

.. qed::

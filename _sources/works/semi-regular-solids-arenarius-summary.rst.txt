Semi-Regular Solids, *Stomachion* & *Sand-Reckoner* — Analytical Summary
========================================================================

*Scope*: **Pappus V.19** · **Heiberg Vol. II, pp. 242–291, 416–424, 446–455** · **Heath (1897), pp. xxxvi, 221–232, 319–326**

Polyhedral Symmetry, Combinatorial Dissection & Cosmic Exponential Scale
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unit 7 brings together the three treatises in which :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` transcends pure planar and quadric exhaustion to pioneer **uniform polyhedral classification**, **enumerative combinatorics**, **Diophantine Pell arithmetic**, and **heliocentric cosmic scale**:

1. **Thirteen Semi-Regular Polyhedra**: Exhausts all vertex-transitive convex polyhedra with regular faces of 2 or 3 kinds (`Pappus V.19`), splitting cleanly into the **octahedral/cubic family** over :eq:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})` (Solids I–VI), the **icosahedral/dodecahedral golden family** over :eq:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)` (Solids VIII–XII, directly modeled in ``geometor/pacioli`` and ``geometor/divine``), and the two **chiral snub cubics** (Solids VII & XIII).
2. **Stomachion & Cattle Problem**: Exact integer/rational dissection of :eq:`12 \times 12 = 144` into 14 commensurable polygons (`Stomachion`), alongside the monumental Pell equation :eq:`t^2 - 4{,}729{,}494\,u^2 = 1` (`Cattle Problem`).
3. **The Sand-Reckoner (*Arenarius*)**: Unites experimental parallax instrument design (correcting for pupil width!), :person:`Aristarchus's <aristarchus>` heliocentric cosmos, and a two-tier :term:`myriad-octad <myriad-octads>` number tower reaching :eq:`10^{8 \cdot 10^{16}}` with the explicit index law :eq:`10^{m-1}\cdot 10^{n-1} = 10^{(m+n-1)-1}`.

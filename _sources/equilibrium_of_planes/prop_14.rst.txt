Book I, Proposition 14: Concurrency of Medians at Triangle Centroid X(2)
========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 198–201 · Heiberg II, 168–177
   :page: 200

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 14.** It follows at once that the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of any triangle is at the intersection of the lines drawn from any two angles to the middle points of the opposite sides respectively.

Applying :just:`Prop. 13` to two medians :g:`AD` and :g:`BE` yields **Proposition 14**: the three medians concur at the unique barycenter :g:`G` dividing each median in the ratio :eq:`2 : 1` (:cite:`geometor:etc`, Kimberling center ``X(2)`` with homogeneous barycentric coordinates :eq:`1 : 1 : 1`).

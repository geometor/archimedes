Book II, Proposition 10: Centre of Gravity of a Parabolic Frustum
=================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 215

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 10.** If a frustum be cut off from a parabola by two parallel chords :g:`BC`, :g:`DE` bisected by diameter :g:`HK` (with :g:`H` on the smaller chord :g:`DE` and :g:`K` on the larger chord :g:`BC`), its :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` divides :g:`HK` in the exact rational ratio determined by combining :just:`Prop. 8` (parabolic segment centroid) with the remainder law (:just:`Book I, Prop. 8`) and the continued-proportion lemma (:just:`Prop. 9`).

Book II, Proposition 6: Bounded Approach of Inscribed Centroids
===============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 207

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 6.** Given a :term:`parabolic segment <tmema-paraboles>`, it is possible to inscribe in it a rectilinear figure in the known manner such that the distance between the :term:`centres of gravity <kentron-barous>` of the segment and of the inscribed figure is less than any assigned length.

*Proof*: By doubling the subdivisions repeatedly (:just:`Eucl. X. 1`), the ratio of the remaining area to the inscribed figure becomes arbitrarily small, forcing the separation of centroids below any preassigned segment (:just:`Book I, Prop. 8`).

.. qed::

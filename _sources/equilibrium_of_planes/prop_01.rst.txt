Book I, Proposition 1: Equal Weights at Equal Distances
=======================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 190–192 · Heiberg II, 144–153
   :page: 190

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 1.** Weights which balance at equal distances are equal.

*Proof*: If they were unequal, take away from the greater the difference between the two. The remainders would then not balance (:just:`Post. 3`), which contradicts :just:`Post. 1` (equal weights at equal distances balance). Hence the weights must be equal.

.. qed::

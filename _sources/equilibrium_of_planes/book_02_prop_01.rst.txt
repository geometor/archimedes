Book II, Proposition 1: Equilibrium of Equal or Unequal Parabolic Segments
==========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 203

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 1.** If two :term:`parabolic segments <tmema-paraboles>` have equal areas, or if their areas have a given ratio, they balance at equal distances or at distances reciprocally proportional to their areas as plane laminae (:just:`Book I, Props. 6–7`).

*Proof*: Combining *Book I, Props. 6–7* with the quadrature of the parabola (:cite:`archimedes:quadrature-of-parabola`), parabolic segments enter static equilibrium relations as homogeneous plane magnitudes.

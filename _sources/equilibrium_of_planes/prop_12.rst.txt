Book I, Proposition 12: Centres of Gravity in Similar Triangles (II)
====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 194–198 · Heiberg II, 158–169
   :page: 197

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 12.** If two triangles be similar, and the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of one of them lie on the straight line joining an angle to the middle point of the opposite side, the centre of gravity of the other will also lie on the straight line similarly drawn in it.

*Proof*: Since the medians join corresponding vertices to midpoints of homologous sides, they are similarly situated lines in similar triangles (:just:`Prop. 11`, :just:`Post. 5`).

.. qed::

Book I, Proposition 8: Centre of Gravity of a Remainder Magnitude
=================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 194–198 · Heiberg II, 158–169
   :page: 194

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 8.** If :g:`AB` be a magnitude whose :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` is :g:`C`, and :g:`AD` a part of it whose centre of gravity is :g:`F`, then the centre of gravity :g:`G` of the remaining part :g:`DE` lies on :g:`FC` produced so that :eq:`GC : CF = AD : DE`.

*Proof*: Since the whole magnitude balances at :g:`C`, the parts :g:`AD` (at :g:`F`) and :g:`DE` (at :g:`G`) must balance about :g:`C`; by the Law of the Lever (:just:`Props. 6–7`), :g:`G` lies on :g:`FC` produced with :eq:`GC : CF = AD : DE`.

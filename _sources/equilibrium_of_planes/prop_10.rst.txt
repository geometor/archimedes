Book I, Proposition 10: Parallelogram Centre of Gravity at Diagonal Intersection
================================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 194–198 · Heiberg II, 158–169
   :page: 196

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 10 (with Alternative Proof).** In any parallelogram, the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` is the point of intersection of its diagonals.

*Proof*: Applying :just:`Prop. 9` to both pairs of opposite sides places the centre of gravity at the intersection of the two bimedians, which coincides with the intersection of the diagonals (:just:`Eucl. I. 34`). Alternatively, decomposing the parallelogram along a diagonal into two congruent triangles and using :just:`Post. 4` and :just:`Prop. 4` confirms the same midpoint.

.. qed::

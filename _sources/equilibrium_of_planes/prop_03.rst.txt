Book I, Proposition 3: Unequal Weights at Unequal Distances
===========================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 190–192 · Heiberg II, 144–153
   :page: 190

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 3.** Unequal weights will balance at unequal distances, the greater weight being at the lesser distance (and conversely, if two unequal weights balance, the greater weight is at the lesser distance).

*Proof*: Reducing the greater weight to equality with the lesser weight shows by :just:`Post. 1` and :just:`Post. 3` that the equal weights cannot balance at unequal distances, forcing the greater weight to occupy the shorter lever arm.

.. qed::

Book II, Proposition 8: Centre of Gravity of a Parabolic Segment (3 : 2)
========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 209

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 8 (Fundamental Centroid of the Parabola).** If :g:`AO` be the diameter of a :term:`parabolic segment <tmema-paraboles>`, and :g:`G` its :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>`, then :eq:`AG = \frac{3}{2} GO` (that is, :eq:`AG = \frac{3}{5} AO` from the vertex :g:`A` and :eq:`GO = \frac{2}{5} AO` from the base midpoint :g:`O`).

*Proof*: Let :eq:`AG : GO = m : n` identically for every parabolic segment (:just:`Prop. 7`). Decompose the main segment :g:`BAb` (area :eq:`\frac{4}{3}\triangle BAb`, :cite:`archimedes:quadrature-of-parabola`) into its inscribed triangle :g:`\triangle BAb` (centroid at :eq:`\frac{2}{3} AO` from :g:`A`, :just:`Book I, Prop. 14`) and two flanking sub-segments on :g:`AB`, :g:`Ab` (combined area :eq:`\frac{1}{3}\triangle BAb`), whose diameters have length :eq:`\frac{3}{4} AO` measured from the line through :g:`A` and whose centroids also divide their diameters in the identical unknown ratio :eq:`AG : GO`! Taking moments along :g:`AO` via :just:`Book I, Props. 4, 6–8` yields a linear equation in :eq:`AG / AO` whose unique solution is :eq:`AG = \frac{3}{5} AO`, i.e. :eq:`AG : GO = 3 : 2`.

.. qed::

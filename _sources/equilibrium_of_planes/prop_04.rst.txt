Book I, Proposition 4: Centre of Gravity of Two Equal Magnitudes
================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 190–192 · Heiberg II, 144–153
   :page: 191

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 4.** If two equal magnitudes have not the same :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>`, the centre of gravity of both taken together is at the middle point of the straight line joining their centres of gravity.

*Proof*: Suppose the combined centre of gravity lay elsewhere than the midpoint; applying the two equal magnitudes at the endpoints of the segment joining their centres would violate :just:`Post. 1` when supported at the midpoint.

.. qed::

Book I, Proposition 11: Centres of Gravity in Similar Triangles (I)
===================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 194–198 · Heiberg II, 158–169
   :page: 197

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 11.** If two triangles be similar, and two points within them be similarly situated with respect to the triangles, and if one point is the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of the first triangle, the other point is likewise the centre of gravity of the second triangle.

*Proof*: Follows directly from :just:`Post. 5` (similarly situated centres of gravity in similar figures) and the uniqueness of the centre of gravity.

.. qed::

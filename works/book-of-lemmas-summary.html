Book of Lemmas (Liber Assumptorum) — Analytical Summary¶

Scope: Heiberg Vol. II, pp. 426–445 · Heath (1897), pp. 301–318 · Propositions 1–15

Planar Gemstones of Circle Tangency, Curvilinear Quadrature & \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\)¶

Unlike the exhaustion treatises, the Book of Lemmas achieves instantaneous exact quadratures of curvilinear figures (Arbelos in Prop. 4, Salinon in Prop. 14) by algebraic cancellation of semicircular areas (Eucl. XII. 2), alongside projective/homothetic circle-contact invariants (Props. 1, 5, 6), kinematic neusis angle trisection (Prop. 8), orthogonal-chord quadrance conservation (Prop. 11), and golden-ratio decagon geometry (Prop. 15).

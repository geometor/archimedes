Bibliography
============

Archival editions, ancient mathematical commentaries, and GEOMETOR companion codices
referenced via the ``:cite:`` role throughout the *Works* and *Method* of Archimedes.

Ancient & Medieval Mathematical Witnesses
-----------------------------------------

.. _cite-archimedes:sphere-and-cylinder:

* **[archimedes:sphere-and-cylinder]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *On the Sphere and Cylinder (Περὶ σφαίρας καὶ κυλίνδρου, Books I–II)* (Heiberg I, 1–255; Heath 1897, pp. 1–90).
  Establishes convexity assumptions, Archimedes' Lemma, and the 2:3 sphere-to-cylinder volume and surface theorem.

.. _cite-archimedes:sphere-and-cylinder:ii-2:

* **[archimedes:sphere-and-cylinder:ii-2]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *On the Sphere and Cylinder, Book II, Prop. 2* (Heiberg I, 194–203; Heath 1897, pp. 59–62).
  Cubature of any spherical segment reduced to an equivalent cone.

.. _cite-archimedes:conoids-and-spheroids:

* **[archimedes:conoids-and-spheroids]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *On Conoids and Spheroids (Περὶ κωνοειδέων καὶ σφαιροειδέων)* (Heiberg I, 272–499; Heath 1897, pp. 99–150).
  Quadrature of the ellipse and cubature of paraboloids, hyperboloids, and ellipsoids of revolution.

.. _cite-archimedes:equilibrium-of-planes:i-7:

* **[archimedes:equilibrium-of-planes:i-7]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *On the Equilibrium of Planes (Ἐπιπέδων ἰσορροπιῶν), Book I, Prop. 7* (Heiberg II, 154–159; Heath 1897, pp. 193–194).
  Proof of the Law of the Lever for incommensurable magnitudes via Eudoxean approximation.

.. _cite-archimedes:quadrature-of-parabola:

* **[archimedes:quadrature-of-parabola]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *Quadrature of the Parabola (Τετραγωνισμὸς παραβολῆς)* (Heiberg II, 294–353; Heath 1897, pp. 233–252).
  Dual mechanical (Props. 1–17) and geometric exhaustion (Props. 18–24) proof that a parabolic segment equals 4/3 of its inscribed triangle.

.. _cite-archimedes:liber-assumptorum:

* **[archimedes:liber-assumptorum]** — Archimedes of Syracuse (tr. Thābit ibn Qurra), *Book of Lemmas (Liber Assumptorum / Kitāb al-Maʾkhūdhāt)* (Heiberg II, 426–445; Heath 1897, pp. 301–318).
  Fifteen planar lemmas preserving the Arbelos, Twin Circles, Neusis Trisection, Salinon, and golden-ratio pentagon/decagon secants.

.. _cite-archimedes:methodus:

* **[archimedes:methodus]** — Archimedes of Syracuse, *The Method of Mechanical Theorems Addressed to Eratosthenes (Ἔφοδος)* (Heiberg II (2nd ed.), 426–507; Heath 1912, pp. 12–51).
  Palimpsest treatise unveiling Archimedes' analytic mechanical slice method and proving the cubature of the cylindrical ungula and bicylinder.

.. _cite-euclid:elements:

* **[euclid:elements]** — Euclid of Alexandria, *Elements (Στοιχεῖα, Books I–XIII)* (Heath ed. 1908 (geometor/euclid & geometor/elements)).
  Axiomatic foundation presupposed throughout Archimedes' canon: especially I.47 (Pythagoras), III.31 & III.35 (chords & semicircles in Arbelos/Salinon), V.Def.4 (Eudoxean ratio), VI.3 (angle bisector in Measurement Prop. 3), X.1 (exhaustion lemma), and XII.2, 7, 10, 18 (circles, pyramids, cones, spheres).

.. _cite-eutocius:in-archimedem:

* **[eutocius:in-archimedem]** — Eutocius of Ascalon, *Commentarii in libros De Sphaera et Cylindro, In Dimensionem Circuli, In libros De Planorum Aequilibriis* (Heiberg Vol. III (Teubner, 1881 / 1915)).
  Sole ancient Greek commentary on Archimedes: supplies explicit numerical multiplications for Measurement Prop. 3, preserves Archimedes' lost cubic-section lemma for Sphere and Cylinder II.4, and records twelve classical solutions to two mean proportionals.

.. _cite-pappus:synagoge:iv:

* **[pappus:synagoge:iv]** — Pappus of Alexandria, *Mathematical Collection (Συναγωγή), Book IV* (Props. 14–25 & 36–44 (ed. Hultsch, Vol. I, 1876)).
  Preserves the 'ancient proposition' on the infinite chain of tangent circles in the Arbelos (cn = n·dn), studies the Archimedean spiral, and classifies solid vs. neusis angle trisections.

.. _cite-pappus:synagoge:v:

* **[pappus:synagoge:v]** — Pappus of Alexandria, *Mathematical Collection (Συναγωγή), Book V, Prop. 19* (Hultsch Vol. I, pp. 350–358 (1876)).
  Primary ancient witness recording the exact face counts and polygon species of all thirteen Semi-Regular Solids discovered by Archimedes.

.. _cite-pappus:synagoge:viii:

* **[pappus:synagoge:viii]** — Pappus of Alexandria, *Mathematical Collection (Συναγωγή), Book VIII* (Hultsch Vol. III, pp. 1022–1147 (1878)).
  Extends Archimedes' theory of centers of gravity (κέντρα βαρῶν) and leads directly to the Pappus–Guldin centroid theorem.

.. _cite-heron:metrica:

* **[heron:metrica]** — Heron of Alexandria, *Metrica (Μετρικά, Books I–III)* (ed. Hermann Schöne (Teubner, 1903)).
  Explicitly quotes Archimedes' Measurement of a Circle, Sphere and Cylinder, Equilibrium, and The Method (ungula and bicylinder).

.. _cite-proclus:in-euclidem:

* **[proclus:in-euclidem]** — Proclus Diadochus, *Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements* (Friedlein pp. 63, 110 (geometor/proclus)).
  Cites Archimedes' postulate that the straight line is the shortest of lines having the same extremities (Friedlein 110) and Hiero's crown/ship narratives (Friedlein 63).

GEOMETOR Ecosystem & Modern Axiomatic Concordances
--------------------------------------------------

.. _cite-geometor:euclid:

  Sister Digital Scriptorium edition of Heath's Euclid and computable G-Index dependency DAG.

.. _cite-geometor:etc:

* **[geometor:etc]** — Clark Kimberling / GEOMETOR, *GEOMETOR • etc: Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers* (X(2) Centroid & Homogeneous Barycentric Coordinates (geometor/etc)).
  Archimedes' On the Equilibrium of Planes I.13–14 provides the foundational synthetic proof of X(2) (intersection of medians) and homogeneous barycentric coordinates (1 : 1 : 1).

.. _cite-geometor:pacioli:

* **[geometor:pacioli]** — Luca Pacioli / GEOMETOR, *GEOMETOR • pacioli: De Divina Proportione (1509)* (geometor/pacioli).
  Renaissance reconstruction of the truncated and semi-regular Archimedean polyhedra illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci.

.. _cite-geometor:pappus:

* **[geometor:pappus]** — Pappus of Alexandria / GEOMETOR, *GEOMETOR • pappus: Projective & Classical Configurations from the Synagoge* (geometor/pappus).
  Companion constructive repository linking Pappus's Collection (harmonic conjugates, circle chains, and semi-regular polyhedra) to Archimedes.

.. _cite-geometor:wildberger:

* **[geometor:wildberger]** — N. J. Wildberger / GEOMETOR, *GEOMETOR • wildberger: Divine Proportions & Rational Trigonometry* (Archimedes' Function / Quadrea A(Q1, Q2, Q3) (geometor/wildberger)).
  Formalizes Archimedes' algebraic invariant A(Q1, Q2, Q3) = (Q1 + Q2 + Q3)^2 - 2(Q1^2 + Q2^2 + Q3^2) = 16·Area^2 governing collinearity, Heron/Archimedes triangle area, and rational circle tangency.

.. _cite-hilbert:1899:

* **[hilbert:1899]** — David Hilbert / GEOMETOR, *Grundlagen der Geometrie (Foundations of Geometry)* (Axiom Group V: Axiom of Continuity / Archimedes' Axiom (geometor/hilbert)).
  Elevates Archimedes' Lemma (Sphere and Cylinder I Assump. 5) into Axiom V 1 of modern axiomatic geometry and demonstrates non-Archimedean ordered fields.

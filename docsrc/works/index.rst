Table of Contents
-----------------

This **Seven-Unit Synchronized Book Codex** unites the entire classical and Palimpsest canon of :person:`Archimedes of Syracuse <archimedes>` (c. 287–212 BC) from :person:`T. L. Heath's <heath>` Cambridge editions (:cite:`heath:1897`, :cite:`heath:1912`) collated directly against 300 DPI archival scans of :person:`J. L. Heiberg's <heiberg>` critical Teubner Greek and Latin editions (:cite:`heiberg:1880`, :cite:`heiberg:1881`). Every unit provides three synchronized reading tracks: **English** (folio-anchored primary translation with calibrated ``.euclid-graphic`` vector plates), **Summary** (deductive architecture & theorem tables), and **Notes** (Doric/Koine philology, Eutocian scholia, and GEOMETOR cross-corpus concordances).

* **Unit 1 — Measurement of a Circle** (*Κύκλου μέτρησις* · Heiberg I, 258–271 · Heath 1897, pp. 91–98):
  :doc:`Measurement of a Circle (Props. 1–3) <measurement-of-a-circle-en>` — Double :term:`exhaustion <exhaustion>` of circle area by a right triangle (:eq:`A = \frac{1}{2}Cr`) and rational 96-gon bisection bounding :eq:`3\frac{10}{71} < \pi < 3\frac{1}{7}`.
  (:doc:`English <measurement-of-a-circle-en>` · :doc:`Summary <measurement-of-a-circle-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <measurement-of-a-circle-notes>`)

* **Unit 2 — On the Equilibrium of Planes, Books I–II** (*Ἐπιπέδων ἰσορροπιῶν αʹ βʹ* · Heiberg II, 142–241 · Heath 1897, pp. 189–220):
  :doc:`On the Equilibrium of Planes I–II <equilibrium-of-planes-en>` — Seven Postulates of :term:`static equilibrium <isorropia>`, the commensurable and incommensurable :term:`Law of the Lever <antipeponthos>` (I.6–7), the foundational synthetic proof of the triangle :term:`centroid <kentron-barous>` ``X(2)`` (I.13–14, :cite:`geometor:etc`), and the :eq:`3 : 2` centroid of the parabolic segment (II.8).
  (:doc:`English <equilibrium-of-planes-en>` · :doc:`Summary <equilibrium-of-planes-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <equilibrium-of-planes-notes>`)

* **Unit 3 — Quadrature of the Parabola** (*Τετραγωνισμὸς παραβολῆς* · Heiberg II, 294–353 · Heath 1897, pp. 233–252):
  :doc:`Quadrature of the Parabola (Props. 1–24) <quadrature-of-parabola-en>` — Epistle to :person:`Dositheus <dositheus>`, :term:`Archimedes' Lemma <archimedes-lemma>`, mechanical balance quadrature (Props. 1–17), and geometric :eq:`1/4^n` exhaustion proving :eq:`\text{Segment} = \frac{4}{3}\triangle ABC` (Props. 18–24).
  (:doc:`English <quadrature-of-parabola-en>` · :doc:`Summary <quadrature-of-parabola-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <quadrature-of-parabola-notes>`)

* **Unit 4 — Sphere and Cylinder, Spirals & Conoids** (*Περὶ σφαίρας καὶ κυλίνδρου · Περὶ ἑλίκων · Περὶ κωνοειδέων καὶ σφαιροειδέων* · Heiberg I & II · Heath 1897, pp. 1–90, 99–188):
  :doc:`Sphere and Cylinder, On Spirals & Conoids and Spheroids <sphere-spirals-conoids-en>` — Convexity postulates (:term:`ἐπὶ τὰ αὐτὰ κοῖλα <concave-same-way>`), surface :eq:`4\pi r^2` and the :eq:`2 : 3` Tomb Theorem of the sphere and cylinder, Eutocius's cubic intersection for II.4, kinematic :term:`spiral <helix>` :term:`subtangent <subtangent>` rectification and :eq:`1/3` sector quadrature, and cubature of :term:`paraboloids <orthogonion-konoeides>`, :term:`hyperboloids <amblygonion-konoeides>`, and :term:`spheroids <sphairoeides>`.
  (:doc:`English <sphere-spirals-conoids-en>` · :doc:`Summary <sphere-spirals-conoids-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <sphere-spirals-conoids-notes>`)

* **Unit 5 — Book of Lemmas (*Liber Assumptorum*)** (*Kitāb al-Maʾkhūdhāt* · Heiberg II, 426–445 · Heath 1897, pp. 301–318):
  :doc:`Book of Lemmas (Propositions 1–15) <book-of-lemmas-en>` — Complete 15-proposition planar treasury preserved by :person:`Thābit ibn Qurra <thabit-ibn-qurra>`: the :term:`Arbelos <arbelos>` (Prop. 4), :term:`Archimedes' Twin Circles <twin-circles>` (Prop. 5), the :eq:`6 : 19` inscribed circle and :person:`Pappus's <pappus>` infinite circle chain (Prop. 6), :term:`neusis <neusis>` angle trisection (Prop. 8), orthogonal-chord quadrance invariant (Prop. 11), the :term:`Salinon <salinon>` (Prop. 14), and golden-ratio pentagon/decagon secants (Prop. 15).
  (:doc:`English <book-of-lemmas-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-of-lemmas-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-of-lemmas-notes>`)

* **Unit 6 — The Method of Mechanical Theorems (*Ephodos*)** (*Περὶ τῶν μηχανικῶν θεωρημάτων πρὸς Ἐρατοσθένην ἔφοδος* · Heiberg 1907 / II², 426–507 · Heath 1912, pp. 12–51):
  :doc:`The Method Addressed to Eratosthenes (Props. 1–15) <method-of-mechanical-theorems-en>` — Recovered by :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>` from the Constantinople Palimpsest in 1906: Archimedes' epistle to :person:`Eratosthenes <eratosthenes>` crediting :person:`Democritus <democritus>` and :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>`, slice-balancing discovery of the parabola and sphere, and cubature of the :term:`cylindrical ungula <ungula>` (Props. 12–14) and :term:`bicylinder <bicylinder>` (Prop. 15).
  (:doc:`English <method-of-mechanical-theorems-en>` · :doc:`Summary <method-of-mechanical-theorems-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <method-of-mechanical-theorems-notes>`)

* **Unit 7 — Thirteen Semi-Regular Solids, *Stomachion* & *The Sand-Reckoner*** (*Ψαμμίτης · Στομάχιον · Τρισκαίδεκα στερεά* · Heiberg II, 242–291 · Heath 1897, pp. xxxvi, 221–232 · Pappus V.19):
  :doc:`Semi-Regular Solids, Stomachion & The Sand-Reckoner <semi-regular-solids-arenarius-en>` — Complete structural concordance of the :term:`13 Archimedean Polyhedra <semi-regular-polyhedra>` with ``geometor/pacioli`` and :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`, the 14-piece :term:`Stomachion <stomachion>` dissection, and *The Sand-Reckoner* to :person:`King Gelon <gelon>` preserving :person:`Aristarchus's <aristarchus>` heliocentric cosmos and the :term:`myriad-octad <myriad-octads>` exponential calculus up to :eq:`10^{8 \cdot 10^{16}}`.
  (:doc:`English <semi-regular-solids-arenarius-en>` · :doc:`Summary <semi-regular-solids-arenarius-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <semi-regular-solids-arenarius-notes>`)

Greek–Latin–English Technical Glossary
======================================

Lexicon of Syracusan Doric, Hellenistic Koine, Arabic-Latin (*Liber Assumptorum*),
and modern geometric terminology wired to the interactive ``:term:`` role.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 18 16 48

   * - Term Key & Headword
     - Greek / Latin
     - English Sense
     - Mathematical Definition & Loci
   * - ``arbelos`` — **arbelos (ἄρβηλος)**
     - *ἄρβηλος* / *scalprum sutorium*
     - shoemaker's knife
     - Curvilinear plane region bounded by three mutually tangent semicircles with collinear diameters AB = AC + CB on the same side of line AB; its area equals the circle whose diameter is the common internal tangent perpendicular CD (Book of Lemmas Prop. 4). (*Loci*: Book of Lemmas Props. 4–6 (Heath 1897, pp. 304–308); Pappus IV.14)
   * - ``twin-circles`` — **Archimedes' Twin Circles**
     - *δίδυμοι κύκλοι* / *circuli aequales in arbelo*
     - Archimedes' Twin Circles
     - The two circles inscribed on opposite sides of the perpendicular CD dividing the arbelos, each tangent to CD, the outer semicircle AB, and one inner semicircle (AC or CB); Archimedes proves they are strictly equal with radius r = (r1·r2)/(r1 + r2) (Book of Lemmas Prop. 5). (*Loci*: Book of Lemmas Prop. 5 (Heath 1897, pp. 305–306))
   * - ``salinon`` — **salinon (σάλινον)**
     - *σάλινον* / *salinum*
     - salt-cellar
     - Symmetric curvilinear figure formed by four semicircles on collinear segments with AD = EB: an outer semicircle AB and central opposite semicircle DE bridged by two inward equal semicircles AD, EB; its area equals the circle on the axial symmetry segment CF (Book of Lemmas Prop. 14). (*Loci*: Book of Lemmas Prop. 14 (Heath 1897, pp. 316–317))
   * - ``neusis`` — **neusis (νεῦσις)**
     - *νεῦσις* / *inclinatio*
     - verging / marked-straightedge insertion
     - Geometric construction inserting a line segment of given length between two given curves or lines such that the extended line 'verges' (νεύει) toward a given point; used by Archimedes to trisect an arbitrary angle (Book of Lemmas Prop. 8) and construct spiral tangents (On Spirals Props. 5–9). (*Loci*: On Spirals Props. 5–9 (pp. 156–161); Book of Lemmas Prop. 8 (p. 309))
   * - ``kentron-barous`` — **kentron tou bareos (κέντρον τοῦ βάρεος)**
     - *κέντρον τοῦ βάρεος* / *centrum gravitatis*
     - center of gravity / centroid
     - Unique point of a plane figure or solid body such that if suspended therefrom it remains in equilibrium in any orientation; proved in Equilibrium of Planes I.13–14 to coincide for any triangle with the concurrency of its medians (Kimberling center X(2)). (*Loci*: Equilibrium I Post. 1–7, Props. 13–14 (pp. 189–202); Method Postulates)
   * - ``isorropia`` — **isorropia (ἰσορροπία)**
     - *ἰσορροπία* / *aequilibrium*
     - static equilibrium
     - State in which magnitudes suspended at distances along a lever balance one another about a fulcrum; governed by Archimedes' seven postulates of plane statics. (*Loci*: Equilibrium of Planes I.Post.1–7, Props. 1–7 (pp. 189–194))
   * - ``antipeponthos`` — **antipeponthos (ἀντιπεπονθέναι)**
     - *ἀντιπεπονθέναι* / *reciproce proportionalia*
     - inverse proportionality (Law of the Lever)
     - Fundamental law proved for both commensurable (I.6) and incommensurable (I.7) magnitudes: two magnitudes balance at distances reciprocally proportional to their weights (A : B = d_B : d_A). (*Loci*: Equilibrium of Planes I.6–7 (pp. 192–194); Method Prop. 1)
   * - ``ephodion`` — **ephodos / ephodion (ἔφοδος / ἐφόδιον)**
     - *ἔφοδος* / *methodus mechanica*
     - heuristic mechanical method
     - Archimedes' analytic art of investigating unknown areas, volumes, and centroids by decomposing figures into parallel line or plane elements and balancing them on a virtual lever against known comparison solids prior to synthetic proof by exhaustion. (*Loci*: The Method (Heath 1912, pp. 12–51; Heiberg II, 426–507))
   * - ``archimedes-lemma`` — **Archimedes' Lemma (Axiom of Archimedes)**
     - *λῆμμα Ἀρχιμήδους* / *axioma Archimedis*
     - Axiom of Archimedes
     - Of unequal lines, unequal surfaces, and unequal solids, the greater exceeds the less by such a magnitude as, when added to itself, can be made to exceed any assigned magnitude among those which are comparable with one another (credited by Archimedes to earlier geometers / Eudoxus; formalized as Hilbert Axiom V 1). (*Loci*: Sphere & Cyl. I Assump. 5 (p. 4); Spirals Pref.; Quadrature Pref. (p. 234))
   * - ``concave-same-way`` — **epi ta auta koila (ἐπὶ τὰ αὐτὰ κοῖλα)**
     - *ἐπὶ τὰ αὐτὰ κοῖλα* / *ad easdem partes concava*
     - convexity / monotonic curvature condition
     - Rigorous geometric definition of convexity for curves and surfaces in Sphere and Cylinder I: a curve or surface is concave in the same direction if the segment joining any two points on it lies wholly on one side (or on) the curve/surface, guaranteeing that an included convex perimeter/surface is strictly less than the enveloping one. (*Loci*: Sphere & Cylinder I Defs. & Assump. 1–4 (Heath 1897, pp. 2–4))
   * - ``exhaustion`` — **Method of Exhaustion**
     - *δι᾽ ἐγγραφομένων καὶ περιγραφομένων* / *methodus exhaustionis*
     - method of exhaustion
     - Rigorous Greek proof paradigm compressing an unknown curvilinear magnitude X between inscribed and circumscribed rectilinear/polygonal sequences (P_n < X < Q_n with Q_n - P_n < ε or Q_n / P_n < 1 + ε) and refuting both X > M and X < M by double reductio ad absurdum. (*Loci*: Measurement Prop. 1; Quadrature Props. 20–24; Sphere & Cyl. I.33–34)
   * - ``helix`` — **helix (ἕλιξ)**
     - *ἕλιξ* / *linea spiralis*
     - Archimedean spiral
     - Plane kinematic locus traced by a point moving uniformly along a straight ray while the ray revolves uniformly in a plane about its fixed extremity O (polar equation r = aθ). (*Loci*: On Spirals Def. 1 & Props. 12–28 (Heath 1897, pp. 151–188))
   * - ``subtangent`` — **polar subtangent of the spiral**
     - *ἡ πρὸς ὀρθὰς τᾷ ἀρχᾷ τᾶς περιφορᾶς* / *subtangens spiralis*
     - spiral subtangent
     - Segment cut off on the perpendicular to the radius vector at the origin by the tangent to the spiral; in On Spirals Props. 18–20 Archimedes proves that at the end of the n-th turn its length equals n times the circumference of the corresponding circle, achieving the kinematic rectification of the circle. (*Loci*: On Spirals Props. 18–20 (Heath 1897, pp. 171–176))
   * - ``tmema-paraboles`` — **tmema orthogoniou konou tomes (τμῆμα ὀρθογωνίου κώνου τομῆς)**
     - *τμῆμα ὀρθογωνίου κώνου τομῆς* / *segmentum parabolae*
     - segment of a parabola
     - Region comprehended by a straight chord AC and a 'section of a right-angled cone' (Archimedes' pre-Apollonian term for a parabola); its area equals 4/3 of the inscribed triangle of greatest area having the same base AC and vertex B where the tangent is parallel to AC. (*Loci*: Quadrature Props. 17, 24 (pp. 246, 252); Method Prop. 1 (1912, p. 15))
   * - ``orthogonion-konoeides`` — **orthogonion konoeides (ὀρθογώνιον κωνοειδές)**
     - *ὀρθογώνιον κωνοειδές* / *conoides rectangulum*
     - paraboloid of revolution
     - Solid generated by revolving a parabola about its axis; Archimedes proves any segment cut off by a plane equals 3/2 of the cone having the same base and axis (half of the enveloping cylinder). (*Loci*: On Conoids and Spheroids Props. 21–22 (pp. 131–134); Method Prop. 4)
   * - ``amblygonion-konoeides`` — **amblygonion konoeides (ἀμβλυγώνιον κωνοειδές)**
     - *ἀμβλυγώνιον κωνοειδές* / *conoides obtusangulum*
     - hyperboloid of revolution
     - Solid generated by revolving one branch of a hyperbola ('section of an obtuse-angled cone') about its transverse diameter. (*Loci*: On Conoids and Spheroids Props. 25–26 (pp. 137–140); Method Prop. 5)
   * - ``sphairoeides`` — **sphairoeides (σφαιροειδές)**
     - *σφαιροειδές* / *sphaeroides*
     - prolate or oblate spheroid
     - Solid generated by revolving an ellipse ('section of an acute-angled cone') about its major axis (elongate/prolate spheroid, παραμᾶκες) or minor axis (flat/oblate spheroid, ἐπιπλατύ). (*Loci*: On Conoids and Spheroids Defs. & Props. 27–32 (pp. 101, 140–150); Method Prop. 3)
   * - ``bicylinder`` — **bicylinder (Steinmetz solid)**
     - *τὸ ἐξ ἀμφοτέρων τῶν κυλίνδρων κοινὸν σχῆμα* / *solidum commune duorum cylindrorum*
     - Archimedean bicylinder
     - Solid common to two right circular cylinders inscribed in a cube with perpendicular intersecting axes; in the concluding theorem of The Method Archimedes discovers and proves that its volume equals 2/3 of the cube (16/3 r³), matching an exclusively planar cross-sectional square construction. (*Loci*: The Method Preface & Prop. 15 (Heath 1912, pp. 14, 46–51))
   * - ``ungula`` — **cylindrical ungula (hoof)**
     - *τμῆμα κυλίνδρου* / *ungula cylindrica*
     - cylindrical hoof / wedge
     - Solid cut from a right circular cylinder inscribed in a rectangular prism by a plane through a diameter of one base and a parallel edge of the opposite face; Archimedes proves both mechanically (Prop. 12–13) and purely geometrically via triangular cross-sections (Prop. 14) that its volume is 1/6 of the prism (2/3 r²h). (*Loci*: The Method Props. 12–14 (Heath 1912, pp. 38–46))
   * - ``semi-regular-polyhedra`` — **Thirteen Archimedean Semi-Regular Solids**
     - *τρισκαίδεκα σχήματα ἰσόπλευρα μὲν καὶ ἰσογώνια, οὐχ ὅμοια δέ* / *corpora semiregularia Archimedis*
     - Archimedean solids
     - The complete finite family of 13 convex non-prismatic, non-Platonic polyhedra bounded by regular polygons of two or three species meeting in congruent vertex configurations, discovered by Archimedes and catalogued in Pappus's Synagoge V.19. (*Loci*: Pappus Synagoge V.19; Heath 1897 Ch. V; Pacioli De Divina Proportione)
   * - ``myriad-octads`` — **myriad-octads (μυριάδες / ὀκτάδες)**
     - *ἀριθμοὶ πρῶτοι καὶ δεύτεροι / περίοδοι* / *ordines et periodi numerorum*
     - Archimedean octad exponential notation
     - Positional naming system constructed in The Sand-Reckoner taking 10^8 (a myriad myriads) as the base of successive 'orders' up to the (10^8)-th order (First Period = 10^(8·10^8)) and iterating through 10^8 Periods up to 10^(8·10^16), accompanied by the law of exponents A_m · A_n = A_(m+n-1). (*Loci*: Sand-Reckoner III.1–6 (Heath 1897, pp. 227–230))
   * - ``stomachion`` — **Stomachion / Ostomachion (Στομάχιον)**
     - *Στομάχιον* / *Loculus Archimedius*
     - Archimedes' Stomachion
     - Combinatorial and areal dissection of a 12 × 12 square into 14 rational-area polygonal tiles (areas 3, 6, 9, 12, 14, 21 out of 144) admitting 17,152 (536 distinct modulo dihedral symmetry) square assemblies. (*Loci*: Heiberg Vol. II (1913, pp. 416–424); Palimpsest fol. 177)
   * - ``quadrea`` — **Archimedes' Function / Quadrea**
     - *τετραπλάσιον ἐμβαδόν* / *functio Archimedis*
     - quadrea A(Q1, Q2, Q3)
     - Symmetric quadratic polynomial A(Q1, Q2, Q3) = (Q1 + Q2 + Q3)^2 - 2(Q1^2 + Q2^2 + Q3^2) = 16·Area^2 in the three side quadrances of a triangle (attributed to Archimedes via Arabic Heron tradition and central to Rational Trigonometry in geometor/wildberger). (*Loci*: geometor/wildberger; Book of Lemmas & Heron Metrica I.8)

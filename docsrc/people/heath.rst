Sir Thomas Little Heath
=======================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Thomas Little Heath*
* **Role**: Editor & translator of The Works of Archimedes (1897) and The Method (1912)
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Passim (Heath 1897 & 1912)

Produced the monumental Cambridge editions of Archimedes (1897, Supplement 1912), Apollonius (1896), and Euclid's Elements (1908, geometor/euclid), combining philological fidelity to Heiberg with crystalline geometric clarity.

Eudoxus of Cnidus
=================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Εὔδοξος ὁ Κνίδιος*
* **Role**: Founder of the general theory of proportions & method of exhaustion
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Sphere & Cyl. I Pref. (p. 2); Quadrature Pref. (p. 234); Method Pref. (1912, p. 13)

Formulated the Lemma of Continuity (Euclid V.Def.4, X.1; Archimedes Sphere & Cylinder I Assump. 5) and gave the first rigorous geometric proofs by exhaustion that a cone is one-third of a cylinder and a pyramid one-third of a prism, praised in Archimedes' prefaces to Sphere and Cylinder I and The Method.

Giovanni Alfonso Borelli & Abraham Ecchellensis)
================================================

* **Original Name / Form**: *Iohannes Alphonsus Borellius*
* **Role**: Editors of the 1661 Florence editio princeps of Liber Assumptorum
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Book of Lemmas Intro (Heath 1897, pp. xxxii, 301)

Translated a 10th-century Arabic manuscript of Thābit ibn Qurra's recension (with al-Nasawī's commentary) at the Medici Library in Florence, publishing Archimedes' Liber Assumptorum in Latin in 1661.

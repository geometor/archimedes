Conon of Samos
==============

* **Original Name / Form**: *Κόνων ὁ Σάμιος*
* **Role**: Court astronomer at Alexandria & closest mathematical confidant of Archimedes
* **Archimedean Witness & Loci**: Sphere & Cyl. I Pref. (p. 1); Spirals Pref. (p. 151); Quadrature Pref. (p. 233)

Celebrated with deep affection by Archimedes for his extraordinary geometric insight; Archimedes sent his theorems on spirals, spheres, and conoids first to Conon, mourning his premature death in the prefaces to Quadrature of the Parabola, Sphere and Cylinder I, and On Spirals.

Book II, Proposition 2: Centre of Gravity of Parabolic Segment Lies on Diameter
===============================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 204

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 2.** If a triangle :g:`\triangle ABC` be inscribed in a :term:`parabolic segment <tmema-paraboles>` in the known manner (with base :g:`BC` and vertex :g:`A`), and two further triangles :g:`\triangle ADB`, :g:`\triangle BEC` be inscribed in the remaining segments, and so on recursively, the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of the whole segment lies on its diameter :g:`AO`.

*Proof*: Each inscribed polygonal tier is symmetric in moment about the diameter :g:`AO`, and by exhaustion (:just:`Eucl. X. 1`) the segment's centre of gravity cannot lie off :g:`AO`.

.. qed::

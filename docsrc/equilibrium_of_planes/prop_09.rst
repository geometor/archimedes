Book I, Proposition 9: Parallelogram Centre of Gravity on Bimedians
===================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 194–198 · Heiberg II, 158–169
   :page: 195

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 9.** In any parallelogram (*Παντὸς παραλληλογράμμου τὸ κέντρον τοῦ βάρεός ἐστιν...*, Heiberg II, 162), the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` lies on the straight line joining the middle points of opposite sides.

*Proof*: Dividing the parallelogram into arbitrarily narrow strips parallel to the two opposite sides, each strip has its centre of gravity on the bimedian, and by an exhaustion reductio via :just:`Prop. 8` and :just:`Post. 4` the centre of gravity of the whole parallelogram cannot lie off that line.

.. qed::

Book II, Proposition 4: Diameter-Division Invariance of Inscribed Polygons
==========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 206

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 4.** The :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of any inscribed exhaustion polygon in a :term:`parabolic segment <tmema-paraboles>` lies on the diameter and approaches the segment's centre of gravity as the number of subdivisions increases.

*Proof*: Applying :just:`Book I, Prop. 8` to the difference between the parabolic segment and the inscribed polygon bounds the separation of their centres of gravity along the diameter.

.. qed::

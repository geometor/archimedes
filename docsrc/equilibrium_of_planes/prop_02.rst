Book I, Proposition 2: Unequal Weights at Equal Distances
=========================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 190–192 · Heiberg II, 144–153
   :page: 190

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 2.** Unequal weights at equal distances will not balance, but will incline towards the greater weight.

*Proof*: Taking away the difference from the greater weight makes the remaining equal weights balance (:just:`Post. 1`). Restoring the removed difference causes the balance to incline towards the greater weight (:just:`Post. 2`).

.. qed::

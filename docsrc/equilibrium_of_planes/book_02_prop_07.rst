Book II, Proposition 7: Constant Diameter Ratio Across All Parabolic Segments
=============================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 208

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 7.** If two :term:`parabolic segments <tmema-paraboles>` have their :term:`centres of gravity <kentron-barous>` on their respective diameters, the centres of gravity divide the two diameters in the **same ratio** across all parabolic segments (whether right or oblique).

*Proof*: Demonstrated via double indirect exhaustion using :just:`Props. 3–6`: any inequality in the division ratios would be contradicted by inscribing sufficiently fine exhaustion polygons whose centroids divide their diameters in identical ratios.

.. qed::

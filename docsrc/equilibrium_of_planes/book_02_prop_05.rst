Book II, Proposition 5: Inscribed Polygon Centroid Closer to Base than Segment
==============================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 207

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 5.** If a rectilinear figure be inscribed in a :term:`parabolic segment <tmema-paraboles>` in the known manner, the :term:`centre of gravity <kentron-barous>` of the whole segment is nearer to the vertex of the segment than is the centre of gravity of the inscribed figure.

*Proof*: Because the remaining small sub-segments have centres of gravity closer to the vertex :g:`A` than the inscribed triangles do, removing them shifts the residual centroid toward the base :g:`BC` (:just:`Book I, Prop. 8`).

.. qed::

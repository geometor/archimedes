Book II, Proposition 3: Exhaustion Polygons of Equal-Base Parabolic Segments
============================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 203–220 · Heiberg II, 182–241
   :page: 205

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 3.** If two :term:`parabolic segments <tmema-paraboles>` have equal bases and equal heights, and rectilinear figures be inscribed in them in the known manner with the same number of sides, the centres of gravity of the two inscribed figures divide their respective diameters in the same ratio.

*Proof*: Pairing corresponding inscribed triangles at each stage of the dyadic subdivision preserves equal ratios of sub-areas and median divisions along both diameters.

.. qed::

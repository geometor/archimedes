Proposition 1: Bisection of Parallel Chords by Parabola Diameter
================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 235–246 · Heiberg II, 298–335
   :page: 235

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 1.** If from a point on a parabola a straight line be drawn which is either itself the axis or parallel to the axis, as :g:`PV`, and if :g:`QQ'` be a chord parallel to the tangent to the parabola at :g:`P` and meeting :g:`PV` in :g:`V`, then :eq:`QV = VQ'`. Conversely, if :eq:`QV = VQ'`, the chord :g:`QQ'` will be parallel to the tangent at :g:`P`.

Drawing on the pre-Apollonian *Elements of Conics* (:person:`Euclid <euclid>` and :person:`Aristaeus <aristaeus>`), Archimedes assumes this fundamental diameter-chord symptomata as known from the *Conics*.

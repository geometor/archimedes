Proposition 8: Lever Bounds for a Right-Angled Trapezium Strip (I)
==================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 240

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 8.** If a triangle :g:`BDC` suspended from :g:`OB` (with :eq:`OA = OB`) be cut by a vertical line into a smaller triangle and a trapezium :g:`FDEC` lying between verticals through :g:`E` and :g:`C`, and if an area :g:`P` hung at :g:`A` balances the trapezium *in situ*, then:

   .. math::

      OA : OE > \text{Trapezium } FDEC : P > OA : OC.

Establishes the fundamental upper and lower lever inequalities for a single vertical trapezium strip cut from :g:`\triangle BDG`.

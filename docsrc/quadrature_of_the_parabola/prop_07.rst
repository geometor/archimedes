Proposition 7: Balancing an Obtuse- or Acute-Angled Triangle on a Lever
=======================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 239

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 7.** Extends Proposition 6 to any triangle :g:`BDG` suspended beneath :g:`OB` (whether :g:`\angle BDG` is acute, right, or obtuse) by decomposing or completing it with right-angled triangles on vertical side :g:`BD`: in every case, an area :g:`P = \frac{1}{3} \triangle BDG` hung at :g:`A` balances :g:`\triangle BDG` *in situ*.

.. qed::

Proposition 13: Balancing a Trapezium Cut from an Inclined Triangle (II)
========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 242

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 13.** Extends Proposition 12 to an arbitrary oblique chord :g:`Qq` not perpendicular to the diameter, proving the corresponding lever inequalities for every trapezium strip cut from :g:`\triangle QqT` via Proposition 5.

Proposition 16: Mechanical Quadrature Against the Tangent Triangle
==================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 244–246 · Heiberg II, 330–335
   :page: 244

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 16.** By double indirect :term:`exhaustion <exhaustion>` on the balance beam (using :just:`Props. 14–15` and :term:`Archimedes' Lemma <archimedes-lemma>`), the parabolic segment equals **one-third** of the enveloping tangent triangle :g:`\triangle QqT`.

*Proof*: Suppose the parabolic segment is either greater or less than :eq:`\frac{1}{3} \triangle QqT`. Choosing :eq:`n` large enough that the terminal triangle of :just:`Prop. 14–15` is less than the assumed difference contradicts the lever inequalities for the inscribed or circumscribed rectilinear trapezia.

.. qed::

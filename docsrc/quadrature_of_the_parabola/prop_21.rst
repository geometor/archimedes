Proposition 21: Eightfold Triangle Ratio in Parabolic Subdivision
=================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 246–252 · Heiberg II, 336–353
   :page: 249

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 21.** If :g:`D`, :g:`E` be the vertices of the two remaining segments on :g:`AB` and :g:`BC`, then:

   .. math::

      \triangle ABC = 8 \cdot \triangle ADB = 8 \cdot \triangle BEC, \qquad \text{whence} \qquad \triangle ADB + \triangle BEC = \frac{1}{4} \triangle ABC.

*Proof*: By :just:`Prop. 19`, each sub-segment's diameter-chord intercept is one-fourth of :g:`BV`, while the base is halved, giving :eq:`\triangle ADB = \frac{1}{8} \triangle ABC` and hence :eq:`\frac{1}{4}` for the sum of both secondary triangles.

.. qed::

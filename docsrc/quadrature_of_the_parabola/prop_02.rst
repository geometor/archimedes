Proposition 2: Subtangent Equals Twice Abscissa on Parabola Diameter
====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 235–246 · Heiberg II, 298–335
   :page: 236

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 2.** If in a parabola :g:`QQ'` be a chord parallel to the tangent at :g:`P`, and if a straight line be drawn through :g:`P` which is either itself the axis or parallel to the axis, and which meets :g:`QQ'` in :g:`V` and the tangent at :g:`Q` to the parabola in :g:`T`, then :eq:`PV = PT`.

Together with Proposition 1, this classical conic property establishes the tangent triangle geometry needed for both the mechanical balance proof (Props. 6–17) and the geometric exhaustion (Props. 18–24).

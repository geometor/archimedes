Proposition 3: Ordinate-Squared Symptomata of the Parabola
==========================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 235–246 · Heiberg II, 298–335
   :page: 236

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 3.** If from a point on a parabola a straight line :g:`PV` be drawn parallel to the axis, and from two other points :g:`Q`, :g:`Q'` on the parabola straight lines :g:`QV`, :g:`Q'V'` be drawn parallel to the tangent at :g:`P` meeting :g:`PV` in :g:`V`, :g:`V'`, then:

   .. math::

      QV^2 : Q'V'^2 = PV : PV'.

Assumed as proved in the *Elements of Conics* (:person:`Euclid <euclid>` and :person:`Aristaeus <aristaeus>`), this quadratic-linear relation (:eq:`y^2 = 4px`) underpins the lever intercept ratios of Propositions 4–5 and 18–21.

.. qed::

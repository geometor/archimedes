Proposition 17: Area Equals Four-Thirds of Inscribed Triangle (Mechanical)
==========================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 244–246 · Heiberg II, 330–335
   :page: 245

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 17.** *The area of any segment of a parabola is four-thirds of the triangle which has the same base and equal height.*

*Proof*: Since :eq:`\triangle QqT = 4 \cdot \triangle QPq` (where :g:`P` is the vertex of the segment where the tangent is parallel to :g:`Qq`, by :just:`Prop. 2`), it follows immediately from :just:`Prop. 16` (:eq:`\text{Segment} = \frac{1}{3} \triangle QqT`) that :eq:`\text{Segment} = \frac{4}{3} \triangle QPq`.

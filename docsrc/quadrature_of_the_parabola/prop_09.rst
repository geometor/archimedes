Proposition 9: Lever Bounds for a Right-Angled Trapezium Strip (II)
===================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 238–244 · Heiberg II, 306–329
   :page: 240

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 9.** Extends the lever inequality of Proposition 8 to an arbitrary vertical trapezium suspended between :g:`E` and :g:`C` on the beam :g:`OB` when the upper base is inclined rather than horizontal, bounding its balancing weight :g:`P` at :g:`A` strictly between the moments at the two vertical boundaries :g:`E` and :g:`C`.

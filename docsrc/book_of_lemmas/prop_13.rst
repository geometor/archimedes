Proposition 13: Equal Chord Segments Cut by Diameter Perpendiculars
===================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 311–315 · Heiberg II, 438–442
   :page: 314

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 13 (Equal Chord Segments Cut by Diameter Perpendiculars).** If a diameter :g:`AB` of a circle meet any chord :g:`CD`, not a diameter, in :g:`E`, and if :g:`AM`, :g:`BN` be the perpendiculars let fall from :g:`A`, :g:`B` upon :g:`CD`, then :eq:`CN = DM`.

*Proof*: Dropping the perpendicular :g:`OK` from the centre :g:`O` onto :g:`CD` bisects :g:`CD` (:just:`Eucl. III. 3`), and since :g:`OK \parallel AM \parallel BN` with :eq:`OA = OB`, :g:`K` is likewise the midpoint of :g:`MN`, whence :eq:`CN = DM`.

.. qed::

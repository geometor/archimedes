Proposition 1: Homothetic Chords of Tangent Circles
===================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 301–304 · Heiberg II, 426–430
   :page: 301

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 1 (Homothetic Chords of Tangent Circles).** If two circles touch at :g:`A`, and if :g:`CD`, :g:`EF` be parallel diameters in them, then :g:`ADF` is a straight line (and similarly :g:`ACE` when tangent externally).

*Proof*: Proved by joining the line of centres through the point of contact :g:`A`, using parallel radii and equal alternate interior angles (:just:`Eucl. I. 29`, :just:`Eucl. VI. 32`) to show that the angles at :g:`D` and :g:`A` make :g:`ADF` a single straight line.

Proposition 9: Intersecting Chords Arc-Sum Lemma
================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 311–315 · Heiberg II, 438–442
   :page: 311

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 9 (Intersecting Chords Arc-Sum Lemma).** If in a circle two chords :g:`AB`, :g:`CD` which do not pass through the centre intersect at right angles, then the sum of the opposite arcs :g:`AD + BC` is equal to the sum of the remaining opposite arcs :g:`AC + BD` (each pair equals a semicircle).

*Proof*: Drawing a diameter parallel to one chord and applying equal chords and arcs cut off between parallel chords yields :eq:`\text{arc } AD + \text{arc } BC = \text{arc } AC + \text{arc } BD`.

.. qed::

Proposition 10: Secant & Tangent Segments from an External Point
================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1897), pp. 311–315 · Heiberg II, 438–442
   :page: 311

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 10 (Secant & Tangent Segments from an External Point).** Suppose a circle touches two given lines or is cut by secants from an external point :g:`T`, with tangent chord :g:`AB` and secant chords joining points of contact—establishing chord-tangent segment proportions later connected to Apollonius's *On Contacts* (*Tangencies*).

*Proof*: Demonstrated via similar triangles formed by the tangent-chord angle (:just:`Eucl. III. 32`) and power-of-a-point proportionality (:just:`Eucl. III. 36`).

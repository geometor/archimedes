Proposition 3: Volume of a Spheroid (Ellipsoid of Revolution)
=============================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 21

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 3.** Any :term:`spheroid <sphairoeides>` is four times the cone which has its base equal to the greatest circle of the spheroid (perpendicular to the axis of revolution) and its height equal to half the axis; and the cylinder having the same base and height equal to the whole axis is :eq:`1\frac{1}{2}` times the spheroid.

Applying the slice-lever engine of Proposition 2, Archimedes balances each circular cross-section of the spheroid together with the corresponding section of the auxiliary cone at the far end of the lever against the circular cross-section of the circumscribing cylinder *in situ*, discovering the :eq:`2 : 3` volume ratio.

.. qed::

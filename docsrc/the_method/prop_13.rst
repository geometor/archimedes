Proposition 13: Alternative Slice Balance for the Cylindrical Ungula
====================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 41

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 13.** Establishes the :eq:`1 : 6` prism-to-ungula ratio a second time via parabolic-section chords and vertical prism slices balanced in situ against a triangular prism, preparing the strict geometric exhaustion of Proposition 14.

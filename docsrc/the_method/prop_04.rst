Proposition 4: Volume of a Paraboloid of Revolution
===================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 24

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 4.** Any segment of a :term:`right-angled conoid <orthogonion-konoeides>` (paraboloid of revolution) cut off by a plane at right angles to the axis is **:eq:`1\frac{1}{2}` times** the cone which has the same base as the segment and the same axis.

Slicing the paraboloid and circumscribing cylinder perpendicular to the axis at distance :eq:`x` from the vertex, the parabolic ordinate squared varies linearly with :eq:`x`, so the cylinder's slice at :eq:`x` balanced against the paraboloid's slice transported to the opposite arm yields the exact :eq:`3 : 2` ratio (:eq:`V_{\text{paraboloid}} = \frac{1}{2} V_{\text{cylinder}} = \frac{3}{2} V_{\text{cone}}`).

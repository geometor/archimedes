Proposition 14: Pure Geometric Exhaustion of the Cylindrical Ungula
===================================================================

.. folio:: Heath (1912), pp. 21–51 · Heiberg II², 446–507
   :page: 44

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 14.** Strictly demonstrates geometrically—without mechanical postulates, using inscribed and circumscribed prism elements bounded by parabolic arcs and double indirect :term:`exhaustion <exhaustion>`—that the :term:`cylindrical ungula <ungula>` is **one-sixth** of the circumscribing square prism.

.. qed::

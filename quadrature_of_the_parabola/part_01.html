Propositions 1–5: Conic Symptomata of the Parabola¶

Propositions 16 & 17: First Quadrature Theorem¶

f. Heath (1897), pp. 244–246 · Heiberg II, 330–335 · p. 244

  • Proposition 16: By double indirect exhaustion on the balance beam, the parabolic segment equals one-third of the enveloping tangent triangle triangleQqT.

  • Proposition 17: Since (where P is the vertex of the segment where the tangent is parallel to Qq), it follows immediately that:

    The area of any segment of a parabola is four-thirds of the triangle which has the same base and equal height.

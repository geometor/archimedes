  • Proposition 20: If ABC be a parabolic segment with vertex B, then triangleABC is greater than half the area of the segment ABC (since triangleABC is half of the circumscribed parallelogram formed by the tangent at B and diameters through A, C). Repeated inscription therefore exhausts the segment by Eucl. X. 1!

  • Proposition 21: If D, E be the vertices of the two remaining segments on AB and BC, then:

    \[\triangle ABC = 8 \cdot \triangle ADB = 8 \cdot \triangle BEC, \qquad \text{whence} \qquad \triangle ADB + \triangle BEC = \frac{1}{4} \triangle ABC.\]
